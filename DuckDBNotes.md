* DuckDB is an in-process OLAP database.
* It stores data column-wise and is used to store large amounts of data.
* It works with vectorised processing.
* MVCC and friendlier SQL.
* It has many APIs for Python, Java, and R.
* It also integrates with pandas, Arrow, and Parquet.
* DuckDB is the SQLite for analytics.  



Limits of DuckDB

* Runs on a single machine.
* Single-Player Machine.
* Not for transactions.
* Read-only for concurrent connections.
